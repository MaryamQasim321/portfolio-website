Dental Clinic Management System

Overview

The Dental Clinic Management System is a comprehensive application designed to manage and streamline clinic operations. It allows users to efficiently record and monitor patient appointments, medical records, treatments, and billing. The system ensures secure patient data storage and enhances workflow efficiency, providing a seamless experience for patients, doctors, and administrative staff.

Features

Patient Management: Store and manage patient personal details, medical history, and treatment records.

Appointment Scheduling: Allows patients to book, view, and cancel appointments based on available time slots.

Treatment Tracking: Record treatments performed, including prescriptions, diagnoses, and follow-up care.

Billing and Payments: Generate invoices and process payments with multiple payment methods.

Inventory Management: Keep track of dental supplies, ensuring timely replenishment.

Reporting: Generate and view reports on patient trends, treatment effectiveness, financial transactions, and appointment history.

Usage

Add Patient Records: Enter and update patient details, medical history, and previous treatments.

Schedule Appointments: Manage doctor availability and allow patients to book, reschedule, or cancel appointments.

Track Treatments: Maintain detailed records of treatments, prescriptions, and follow-ups.

Generate Reports: Use the calendar feature to filter and analyze patient history, treatment effectiveness, and financial summaries.
